===== Important Notes for Reproducibility =====

Results Variation Disclaimer:
Although we fix all random seeds, enforce deterministic CUDA settings, 
and control DataLoader randomness, minor variations in results may still occur due to:

1. Hardware differences (GPU architecture, CPU instruction sets).
2. Non-deterministic GPU operations (e.g., attention and memory-related CUDA kernels).
3. Floating-point accumulation and operation order differences across systems.
4. Stochastic data augmentations during training (e.g., random cropping and flipping),
   which are seed-controlled but may still exhibit hardware-dependent behavior.
5. PyTorch / torchvision version differences, including minor (patch-level) updates.

I strongly recommend reproducing results on the same hardware and software environment 
for closest alignment with reported metrics.
